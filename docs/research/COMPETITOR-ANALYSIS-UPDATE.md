## Competitor #1: QuizChain

### Overview
**URL:** https://quizchain.vercel.app/
**Status:** Active (waitlist signup available)
**Blockchain:** Solana

### What It Is
Knowledge competition platform where users compete in quizzes and win Solana rewards instantly.

### Key Features
- ✅ Free to participate (no entry fee)
- ✅ Instant SOL rewards to connected wallet
- ✅ Multiple quiz categories (general knowledge, tech, sports, entertainment, branded)
- ✅ Community can create custom quizzes
- ✅ Prize pools clearly marked
- ✅ Leaderboard system

### Business Model
**For Players:** Free play, win SOL rewards
**For Brands:** Custom branded quizzes for marketing/engagement

### How It Works
1. Sign up + connect Solana wallet
2. Browse available quizzes
3. Compete (answer questions correctly)
4. Get rewarded (instant SOL transfer)

### Strengths
- ✅ Already operational
- ✅ Brand partnerships (revenue model)
- ✅ Free-to-play (low barrier to entry)
- ✅ Multiple quiz types

### Weaknesses
- ❌ Solo play (not head-to-head competitive)
- ❌ No staking mechanism (players don't risk anything)
- ❌ Prize pools creator-funded (not player-funded)
- ❌ Unclear gameplay: is it timed? multiplayer? FFA?

## Competitor #2: Chomp

### Overview
**URL:** https://app.chomp.games/
**Status:** Very Active (multiple grants, hackathon winner)
**Blockchain:** Solana

### What It Is
Social consensus quiz/polling game - "wisdom of the crowd" oracle platform.

### Key Features
- ✅ Quiz, polling, and trivia gameplay
- ✅ "Burn to reveal, reveal to earn" mechanics
- ✅ Free-to-play OR credits-to-play modes
- ✅ $BONK/$GATOR token integration
- ✅ Brands/creators can post questions
- ✅ Users stake tokens to submit answers
- ✅ Rewards based on majority consensus

### Business Model
**Mechanism:** Players stake CHOMP tokens to reveal their answer
**Rewards:** Players who pick the "most likely answer" (consensus) earn rewards
**Oracle Use Case:** Aggregate wisdom of crowd for decentralized truth

### Funding & Traction
- 🏆 **1st Place** (Grand Prize) - Bonkathon Consumer track
- 🏆 **3rd Place** - Colosseum Renaissance Consumer Apps
- 💰 **BONK DAO Grant:** 10 billion $BONK tokens
- 💰 **Build with Monke Grant:** Community engagement funding

### Strengths
- ✅ Proven traction (hackathon wins, grants)
- ✅ Innovative oracle mechanism
- ✅ Token economics (staking, burning)
- ✅ Brand/creator partnerships
- ✅ Dual free/paid modes

### Weaknesses
- ❌ Not truly "competitive" (consensus-based, not skill-based)
- ❌ No direct 1v1 duels
- ❌ Requires understanding token mechanics (barrier)
- ❌ More complex than simple trivia
