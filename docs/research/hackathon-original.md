## Indie.fun Hackathon - $10k+ in prizes (and growing!)

### Overview

This is a 4-week hackathon with a $10,000+ prize pool (and growing), focused on creating fun and unique Solana dapps.

### Prize Structure

- **1st Place:** 5,000 USDC
- **2nd Place:** 3,000 USDC
- **3rd Place:** 2,000 USDC
- Additional bonuses available for integrating partner technology (Moddio, Play Solana)
- Total prize pool subject to growth

### Key Deadlines

- **Submission Deadline:** December 12, 2025 at 11:59 PM EST
- **Winners Announcement:** December 20, 2025

### Project Requirements

Participants must develop "a project (game or dapp) that shows a unique Solana use case."

**Optional Theme:** Building prediction market projects

**Mandatory:** All work must integrate Solana features

### Submission Guidelines

Required submissions include:

1. **Public GitHub repository link** - Must be publicly accessible
2. **Social media presence link** - Twitter account
3. **Compelling video trailer** - Showcasing your project
4. **Indie.fun project page link** - Project must be listed on indie.fun
5. **All content in English**

### Judging Criteria

Evaluation focuses on:

- **Product Quality** - Overall polish and functionality
- **Technical Implementation** - Code quality and Solana integration
- **Originality** - Uniqueness of the concept
- **UX/Design** - User experience and interface design
- **Vision** - Long-term potential and roadmap
- **Social Proof** - Community engagement and traction

### Eligibility

- **Participants:** Solo participants or teams welcome
- **Age Requirement:** 18+ years old
- **Geographic:** Global participants accepted
- **Project Status:** New and existing projects eligible with demonstrable new progress
- **Technical Requirement:** Must integrate Solana features

### Supporting Organizations

Partners providing development support:

- Solana
- Solana Gaming
- Play Solana
- icm.run
- Alphabot
- Solana Superteams

### Partner Bonuses

Additional bonuses available for integrating:

- **Moddio** - Partner technology integration bonus
- **Play Solana** - Partner technology integration bonus
